Selected delivery highlights

Device-facing API at scale

I own delivery for the API that about 700,000 devices connect to every day. I work with DevOps and infrastructure on capacity and scaling on AWS, and act as the API's de facto product owner: priorities, customer-facing changes and how new features land.

Enterprise fleet rollout

I coordinated cloud-side delivery for a large US enterprise customer as its fleet grew to about 110,000 live devices, adding roughly 10,000 a month. I set the delivery approach, guided my team's testing and was the cloud counterpart for the customer.

Marketplace partner integration

I coordinated cloud-side delivery for a major telematics marketplace partner that sells our devices to its customers: integration plans, testing approach, and both teams aligned on scope and timing.

Hardware, firmware and cloud in step

I work with the hardware and firmware team on features, timelines and documentation, so a device capability and its cloud support ship together.

Getting features through the organization

I push features through internal departments, raise risks early and work closely with support so customer issues reach the right team quickly.
